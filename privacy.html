Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Reminda is built so that your reminders stay yours. In short: we don't collect, store or share any of your data. Reminda has no account, no servers and no analytics or advertising.

What stays on your iPhone

Your reminders, notes, settings and the name you may enter for the greeting are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Because they are part of the app's data on your phone, they are included in your own iCloud or computer device backups if you have those turned on, and they are restored with them. That backup is between you and Apple.

Permissions Reminda asks for

  • Notifications: to ring when a reminder is due. Alerts are scheduled on your device and don't go through any server.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: only if you use voice entry, and only while you are adding a reminder. Reminda turns what you say into text. Where your language supports it, recognition happens on your iPhone. If it doesn't, iOS may send the audio to Apple to be transcribed, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Reminda does not keep recordings. Typing always works if you decline.

On-device understanding

Reminda understands phrases like “call Mom in 30 minutes” using Apple's on-device model when your iPhone supports it, and with built-in rules otherwise. Your text is not sent to us or to any third-party service.

Lock Screen and notifications

Live Activities are created and updated by the app on your device. If you hide notification previews or mark a reminder sensitive, Reminda shows generic text instead of the title. Notes are never shown.

Children

Reminda collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
