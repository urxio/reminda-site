Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Minns is built so that your reminders stay yours. In short: we don't collect, store or share any of your data. Minns has no account, no servers and no analytics or advertising.

What stays on your iPhone

Your reminders, notes, settings and the name you may enter for the greeting are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Because they are part of the app's data on your phone, they are included in your own iCloud or computer device backups if you have those turned on, and they are restored with them. That backup is between you and Apple.

Permissions Minns asks for

  • Notifications: to ring when a reminder is due. Alerts are scheduled on your device and don't go through any server.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: only if you use voice entry, and only while you are adding a reminder. Minns turns what you say into text. Where your language supports it, recognition happens on your iPhone. If it doesn't, iOS may send the audio to Apple to be transcribed, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Minns does not keep recordings. Typing always works if you decline.
  • Reminders: only if you choose to import from Apple Reminders, in Settings or when Minns offers it after setup. Minns reads the unfinished reminders in the lists you pick and copies their titles, notes, due dates, repeats and priority into Minns on your iPhone. It never changes or deletes anything in Apple Reminders, and nothing is sent to us. You can turn this access off at any time in iPhone Settings.

On-device understanding

Minns understands phrases like “call Mom in 30 minutes” using Apple's on-device model when your iPhone supports it, and with built-in rules otherwise. Your text is not sent to us or to any third-party service.

Lock Screen, widgets and notifications

Notifications, Live Activities and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets are created by the app on your device. Widgets read a small copy of your upcoming reminders that Minns keeps in its own storage on your iPhone; it is never sent anywhere. Notifications and widgets can show a reminder's title and notes, and Live Activities show its title. If you turn on Hide reminder details in Settings or mark a reminder sensitive, Minns shows generic text instead and leaves the notes out.

Children

Minns collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
