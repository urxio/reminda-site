Support

Need a hand with Minns? Start with the answers below.

I'm not getting notifications

Open Settings → Notifications → Minns on your iPhone and make sure notifications are allowed. If they are off, Minns also shows a notice on the Focus screen with a button that takes you straight there. Check that Focus modes or Do Not Disturb aren't silencing them.

Voice entry isn't working

Voice entry needs Microphone and Speech Recognition permission. Turn them on under Settings → Minns. You can always type instead.

How do I snooze or reschedule?

Press and hold a notification for snooze choices, including Other time… where you can type something like “Friday 3pm”. In the app, tap Reschedule on a reminder.

I got a new iPhone

Minns keeps your reminders on your phone. If you restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or transfer directly from your old one, your reminders come with it and Minns sets up their alerts again the first time you open it. There is no account to sign in to.
