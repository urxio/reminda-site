Reminders for iPhone

A quieter day.

Write a reminder the way you'd say it. Reminda works out the time, rings when it's due, and keeps everything else out of the way.

Coming soon to the App Store

No account. No ads. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Focus

One thing at a time.

Focus shows the next thing that needs you, big and clear. Mark it done with a tap, or move it to later with a few words like “Friday 3pm” or “in 2 hours”.

Natural entry

Type it. Or say it.

“Water the plants every Mon, Wed and Fri at 8am.” Reminda picks out the title, the time and the repeat. Use the microphone if you'd rather talk, and typing always works too.

In control

Check it before it's saved.

A review screen shows exactly what Reminda understood: the title, the date and time, the repeat. Fix anything, then save. Prefer speed? Turn on instant adding in Settings.

Yours

Make it feel like yours.

Add your name to the greeting and choose the app icon that suits your Home Screen. Light and dark follow your iPhone.

Quiet, but it won't let you forget.

Repeats that fit life

Daily, weekly, the first Thursday of the month, every few days, or counted from when you last did it.

Gentle follow-ups

Mark a reminder Important and it nudges again if you haven't dealt with it. It stays silent overnight.

Snooze in a press

Hold a notification to move it to later today, tomorrow or next week, or type a time in your own words.

Lock Screen countdown

A Live Activity counts down to a timed reminder, with Complete and “In an hour” right there.

Early heads-up

Get a nudge 5, 15 or 30 minutes, an hour or a day before.

Private by design

No account, no servers, no tracking. Your reminders live on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
